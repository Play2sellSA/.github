## What is SalesOS

**SalesOS** is a sales operating system that combines **gamification**, **lead management CRM**, and **cash prize payments** into a single platform. We turn sales teams into high-performance engines by making every action count — literally.

Companies use SalesOS to motivate sellers, distribute and track leads, and pay out commissions and prizes — all from one place.

---

## Core Modules

### Gamification

Rankings, missions, quizzes, and PlayCoins that keep sales teams engaged and competing. Badges follow the **Open Badges / IMS 1EdTech** standard, making achievements portable and verifiable. Governance-driven rules let managers design incentive programs that align with business goals.

### Lead Management CRM

Capture leads from any source, distribute them intelligently across your sales team, and track every interaction through the pipeline. Smart distribution rules ensure the right lead reaches the right seller at the right time.

### Pay

Cash prize payments, commission splits, and financial incentives powered by **PIX/TED** and BaaS digital accounts. Automated NFS-e invoicing via Omie ERP. From award calculation to tax-compliant payout — handled end to end.

---

## Key Differentiators

- **Governance-driven gamification** — managers define rules, weights, and reward structures that adapt to each campaign
- **AI-powered insights** — intelligent lead scoring, seller recommendations, and performance analytics
- **API-first architecture** — integrate with your existing stack via REST APIs and webhooks
- **Open Badges standard** — portable, verifiable digital credentials following IMS 1EdTech specifications
